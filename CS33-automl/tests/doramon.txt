Doraemon encyclopedia reference (RAG test edition)
Part I: Core setting and background

1. Robot model and construction
Doraemon is a cat-shaped childcare robot from the 22nd century (born September 3, 2112), model MS-903. He lost both ears in an accident: in 2122 a robot mouse bit them off, which gave him severe musophobia. After losing his ears he cried so much that the tears shook off the yellow paint, revealing blue primer underneath.

2. Energy and performance

Height: 129.3 cm

Weight: 129.3 kg

Horsepower: 129.3 hp

Chest circumference: 129.3 cm

Top speed (when seeing a mouse): 129.3 km/h

Power: internal nuclear fusion reactor (early setting: atomic reactor). He can convert any eaten matter into energy; his favorite food is dorayaki.

Part II: Key characters

Nobita Nobi: protagonist, attends Tsukimidai Elementary in Nerima, Tokyo. Timid and lazy, but gifted at shooting and string figures. In one future he would fail in business and go deep into debt; after history changes, his future wife shifts from Jaiko to Shizuka Minamoto.

Shizuka Minamoto: loves bathing (~3 times per day) and violin (notoriously painful to hear). Favorite food is baked sweet potato, which she often keeps secret because she finds it inelegant.

Takeshi Goda (Gian): nicknamed "Big G," the neighborhood boss. Dreams of being a singer; his singing is treated as a sonic weapon. His motto: "What's yours is mine, and what's mine is still mine."

Suneo Honekawa: rich kid, good at RC models and showing off. Self-interested and somewhat insecure about his height.

Part III: Secret gadgets and principles

Doraemon has over 2000 gadgets stored in his "fourth-dimensional pocket."

1. Space jump: Anywhere Door

Function: think of a destination, open the door, and cross space.

Limit: search radius up to 10 light-years; destination must appear on a map.

2. Time: Time Machine

Entry: inside Nobita's desk drawer.

Size: length 2.2 m, width 1.6 m, height 1.28 m, weight 538 kg. Travels eras via the "time turbulence" tunnel.

3. Logic: What-if Telephone Booth

Principle: a parallel-world creator. Speak a hypothesis into the phone (e.g. "a world without money"); experimental waves switch reality to a matching parallel world.

Restore: call again and say "please restore the world" to undo.

4. Flight: Bamboo Copter

How it works: small battery inside; uses anti-gravity rather than simple prop thrust.

Endurance: up to 8 hours on a full charge, top speed 80 km/h.
