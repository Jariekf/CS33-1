Classification with an Academic Success Dataset


============================================================
Overview
============================================================

Welcome to the 2024 Kaggle Playground Series!

We plan to continue in the spirit of previous playgrounds, providing interesting and approachable datasets for our community to practice their machine learning skills, and anticipate a competition each month.

Your Goal:
The goal of this competition is to predict academic risk of students in higher education.


============================================================
Evaluation
============================================================

Submissions are evaluated using the accuracy score.


============================================================
Submission File
============================================================

For each id row in the test set, you must predict the class value of the Target, which is a categorical academic risk assessment.

The file should contain a header and have the following format:

id,Target
76518,Graduate
76519,Graduate
76520,Graduate
etc.


============================================================
About the Tabular Playground Series
============================================================

The goal of the Tabular Playground Series is to provide the Kaggle community with a variety of fairly light-weight challenges that can be used to learn and sharpen skills in different aspects of machine learning and data science.

The duration of each competition will generally only last a few weeks, and may have longer or shorter durations depending on the challenge.

The challenges will generally use fairly light-weight datasets that are synthetically generated from real-world data, and will provide an opportunity to quickly iterate through various model and feature engineering ideas, create visualizations, etc.


============================================================
Synthetically-Generated Datasets
============================================================

Using synthetic data for Playground competitions allows us to strike a balance between having real-world data with named features and ensuring test labels are not publicly available.

This allows us to host competitions with more interesting datasets than in the past.

While there are still challenges with synthetic data generation, the state-of-the-art is much better now than when we started the Tabular Playground Series two years ago, and that goal is to produce datasets that have far fewer artifacts.

Please feel free to give us feedback on the datasets for the different competitions so that we can continue to improve!

============================================================
Column Descriptions
============================================================

id:
Unique row identifier in the local dataset.

Marital status:
Encoded marital status of the student at enrollment.

Application mode:
Encoded admission or application channel used by the student.

Application order:
Preference order in which the student applied for the course.

Course:
Encoded academic program or degree course.

Daytime/evening attendance:
Indicator of whether the student attends during daytime or evening.

Previous qualification:
Encoded type of qualification held before enrollment.

Previous qualification (grade):
Numeric grade associated with the student's previous qualification.

Nacionality:
Encoded nationality of the student.

Mother's qualification:
Encoded education level of the student's mother.

Father's qualification:
Encoded education level of the student's father.

Mother's occupation:
Encoded occupation category of the student's mother.

Father's occupation:
Encoded occupation category of the student's father.

Admission grade:
Student's admission score or grade at entry.

Displaced:
Binary indicator related to displaced student status.

Educational special needs:
Binary indicator for special educational needs.

Debtor:
Binary indicator showing whether the student has outstanding debt.

Tuition fees up to date:
Binary indicator showing whether tuition payments are current.

Gender:
Encoded gender indicator.

Scholarship holder:
Binary indicator for scholarship support.

Age at enrollment:
Age of the student when entering the program.

International:
Binary indicator for international student status.

Curricular units 1st sem (credited):
Number of first-semester curricular units credited from prior work or transfer.

Curricular units 1st sem (enrolled):
Number of first-semester curricular units enrolled.

Curricular units 1st sem (evaluations):
Number of first-semester evaluations taken.

Curricular units 1st sem (approved):
Number of first-semester curricular units successfully approved.

Curricular units 1st sem (grade):
Average grade across first-semester curricular units.

Curricular units 1st sem (without evaluations):
Number of first-semester curricular units without evaluation records.

Curricular units 2nd sem (credited):
Number of second-semester curricular units credited from prior work or transfer.

Curricular units 2nd sem (enrolled):
Number of second-semester curricular units enrolled.

Curricular units 2nd sem (evaluations):
Number of second-semester evaluations taken.

Curricular units 2nd sem (approved):
Number of second-semester curricular units successfully approved.

Curricular units 2nd sem (grade):
Average grade across second-semester curricular units.

Curricular units 2nd sem (without evaluations):
Number of second-semester curricular units without evaluation records.

Unemployment rate:
Macroeconomic unemployment rate associated with the observation period.

Inflation rate:
Macroeconomic inflation rate associated with the observation period.

GDP:
Macroeconomic GDP-related indicator associated with the observation period.

Target:
Final student academic status. Values observed locally are Graduate, Dropout, and Enrolled.
