============================================================
2. Dataset Purpose
============================================================

The data was collected from students in the Faculty of Engineering and the Faculty of Educational Sciences in 2019.

The purpose of this dataset is to predict students' end-of-term performances using machine learning techniques.


============================================================
3. Additional Information
============================================================

Questions 1 to 10 are personal questions.

Questions 11 to 16 are family-related questions.

The remaining questions are about education habits.
